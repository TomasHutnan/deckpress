## [diamonds] 2. Crazy Eights

**Players:** 2–5 · **Deck:** 52 cards (remove Jokers)

A fast-paced shedding game for all ages. Be the first player to empty your hand!

### Setup
1. Deal **5 cards** to each player (**7 cards** in a 2-player game).
2. Place the remaining deck face down in the center as the **Draw Pile**.
3. Flip the top card face up to start the **Discard Pile**.

### On Your Turn
- Play **1 card** that matches the top discard card's **suit** or **rank**.
- **All {{count:rank=8}} Eights are Wild:** play an `8` on any turn and declare the new active suit — {{color:spades "Spades"}}, {{color:hearts "Hearts"}}, {{color:diamonds "Diamonds"}}, or {{color:clubs "Clubs"}}.
- If you cannot play, **draw** from the Draw Pile until you draw a playable card (or pass if the pile runs empty).

### Scoring (Optional Multi-Round)
When a player empties their hand, they score points from opponents' leftover cards:
- **8s:** 50 pts · **K, Q, J:** 10 pts · **Ace:** 1 pt · **2–10:** face value.
- First player to **100 points** wins the match.
