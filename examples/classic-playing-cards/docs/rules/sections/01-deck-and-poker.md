## [hearts] 1. Texas Hold'em Poker

**Players:** 2–8 · **Deck:** 52 cards (remove Jokers)

1. Deal **2 private hole cards** face down to each player.
2. Bet across four rounds: **Pre-Flop**, **Flop** (3 community cards), **Turn** (+1), and **River** (+1).
3. Make the strongest **5-card hand** from any combination of your 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards.
