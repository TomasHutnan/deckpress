## [clubs] 3. Blackjack (21)

**Players:** 2–7 (1 Dealer) · **Deck:** 52 cards (remove Jokers)

Beat the Dealer by getting a hand total closer to **21** without going over (**Busting**).

### Card Values
- **Number cards (2–10):** Worth their pip value.
- **Court cards (J, Q, K):** Worth **10 points** each.
- **Aces (A):** Worth **11** or **1** — whichever benefits your hand most.

**Natural Blackjack (21 on the first two cards):**

{{card-row:width=18 S_A "11 pts" | + | H_K "10 pts" | -> "21!"}}

### Round Flow
1. Deal **2 cards** to each player (face up) and **2 cards** to the Dealer (1 face up, 1 face down).
2. Each player chooses to **Hit** (take another card) or **Stand** (keep current total). Going over 21 immediately busts.
3. Once all players stand, the Dealer reveals their hole card and **must Hit on 16 or less** and **Stand on 17 or more**.
4. Players with a higher total than the Dealer (or any standing player if the Dealer busts) win the round!
